**Quote and contact requests.** When you use our form we process what you enter: name, company, email, product or service of interest and your message. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You also confirm the consent box before sending.

**Technical data.** Our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type in server logs, in order to deliver the website and protect it against abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Bot protection.** When you start filling in a form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person and not an automated program. This is used only for security.

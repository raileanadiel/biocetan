## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work; others are used to measure or track visitors.

## Cookies on this website

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. This website does not show a cookie banner because it does not set non-essential cookies.

- **Language and navigation:** no cookies. The language is part of the page address.
- **Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile):** loaded only when you start filling in a form. It may store technical data in your browser to tell people from automated programs. It is used only for security.
